# The Cantoria extension

Lily# writes the score; a singing voice is another program's work. **Cantoria** is the
extension that stands between the two: Lily# hands it the notes and the words of each
`singer` line, and it has an engine on your computer sing them.

Cantoria is a separate, free extension (Apache-2.0). It has no editor of its own: once it
is installed you only meet it in the steps that follow, where it is told where an engine is.

Nothing is sent anywhere: the voices sing on this computer.
